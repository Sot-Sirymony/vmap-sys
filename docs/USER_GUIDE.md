# User Guide

## Start

1. Run the backend.
2. Run the frontend.
3. Register a new account or sign in with the demo account.

## Create a Vision Map

1. Open `Vision Areas`.
2. Create a major area such as Career Development.
3. Open `Dreams`.
4. Create a dream under the Vision Area.
5. Open `Goals`.
6. Create goals under the dream.
7. Open `Steps`.
8. Create ordered steps under each goal.
9. Open `Tasks`.
10. Create executable tasks under steps.

### Build my plan (guided)

Instead of the pages above, you can build the plan for one dream in a single guide:

1. Open the dream and choose **Build my plan**.
2. Work through the six stages: Clarify, Goals, Steps, Tasks, Helpers and risks, Check and add.
   Skip any stage, or go back with **Back** or by choosing a stage at the top.
3. Goals and steps already in your plan are shown and kept. You only add what is missing.
4. With AI consent, **Suggest goals** and **Suggest steps** add AI suggestions you can edit or remove.
   Without AI, use **Add a goal**, **Add a step** and **Add a task** with the questions shown at each stage.
   If a question appears, answer it in place, or choose **Suggest anyway**. In Clarify, **Ask me questions first**
   lets the plan builder ask up to three questions about your dream; tick the box to also save an answer into the dream.
   Not happy with the suggestions? Pick **Fewer**, **More**, **Smaller**, **Bolder**, **Earlier** or **Later** and choose
   **Refine**. Suggestions you edited or locked (lock icon) stay as they are. **Another option** swaps one suggestion.
5. Each step can have an estimate in hours and the earlier step it **Starts after**. **Suggest steps for all goals**
   fills in steps for every goal that has none yet.
   Mark a step **Needs several tasks (complex)** to break it into tasks in the Tasks stage, where **Suggest tasks**
   proposes small tasks with owner, due date, priority and hours. They can be refined, locked or swapped like steps.
6. In **Helpers and risks**, **Suggest helpers and risks** proposes the kinds of people the plan needs, matched to
   your own partners, and what could get in the way, with the kind of helper that fits each. Tick
   **Also use my fear journal for this** to let your fears suggest risks. Choose a partner to link, or add a new
   contact to reach. After adding the plan, **Draft a message** writes a note for each new contact to copy and send.
7. Your draft saves as you work. Leave and come back on any device within 14 days and it is still there.
   **Discard draft** removes it without touching your saved plan.
8. In **Check and add**, fix anything listed as needing attention. **Check my draft** runs the plan check before
   saving: fix simple problems in one click, or choose **Fix with AI** to see a proposed change you can apply or not.
   Give the hours a week you have, and the guide flags weeks with too much work; **Spread the dates** moves new work
   later where its step or goal date allows. Then choose **Add plan**.
   Everything is added together, or nothing is. **Undo** right after archives what was just added.
9. Each new goal, step and task has a menu (⋮) to **Move up**, **Move down**, **Merge with the next one** or
   **Split into two** before you add the plan. Merging joins the titles and keeps the later date and higher priority;
   splitting adds an empty item right after it for you to write.
10. One plan session can use up to 15 AI requests; the guide shows how many are used. After that it carries on with the
   questions at each stage. Adding or discarding the draft starts a new session.

After adding dreams from **Suggest dreams**, choose **Build my plan for ...** to go straight into the guide for that dream.

### Plan assistant

On the **Plan** page and in **Build my plan**, the round button at the bottom right opens the plan assistant
instead of the Dream Coach. Each section also has a **Help with this section** button.

- The panel says what it is helping with, what the section needs, why it matters, questions to think about, and
  what the plan check found in that section. This part works even when AI is off.
- In Build my plan it follows you from stage to stage. Tick **Keep this topic when I move to another section** to stay on one.
- With AI consent, pick one of three ready questions or type your own. The assistant sees only that section of your plan.
- **Suggest changes** gives small change cards, such as a clearer title, a missing step or a likely risk. Edit a card
  if you like, then choose **Apply to draft** (Build my plan) or **Save to my plan** (Plan page), or **Dismiss** it.
  Nothing changes unless you choose it.

## Strengths and Gaps

Take an honest look at what you bring and what you lack for one dream. You are not trying
to fix every weakness.

1. Open `Strengths and Gaps` (under Plan). Your top-ranked dream is chosen first.
2. Add strengths first, then gaps. Each needs one real example as evidence.
3. Say how much each matters to the dream. Only items that matter a lot need action.
4. Optional: type in what someone else said, and whether you agree. If you do not, the
   item is marked *Outside view differs*.
5. For each gap that matters a lot, choose one way to cover it:
   - Partner: adds them to your Partners page.
   - Delegate or automate: adds a row to the Multiply Your Effort module.
   - Resource: adds a row to the Close Your Resource Gaps module.
   - Learn it myself: adds a dated step to one of the dream's goals. At most 2 at a time.
   - Accept it: write one line on why it does not matter enough.
6. Use `Save this profile`. After about 12 weeks, look again and use `Save a retake` to
   see what changed. When you remove a gap, you choose whether to keep what it added.

The profile appears on the Plan page after the dream statement and in the plan PDF. It
never stops the plan from being ready. Suggestions from Work Style and low assessment
scores are only saved once you add your own evidence.

## Manage Tasks

The Tasks page shows a board with these columns:
- Not Started
- In Progress
- Waiting
- Blocked
- Completed
- Paused

Use the status select on a task card to move it between columns.

If a task is blocked, enter a blocker reason.

## Page assistant and helpers (V15)

The **Modules**, module, **Assessment**, **Obstacles**, **Communication** and **Work Style** pages have a page assistant.
Use the **Help** button at the top of the page, or a **Help with this** button next to a part of the page (a question,
a form, a message field, a row, a partner). The round button at the bottom right opens the same panel; on these pages it
takes the place of the Dream Coach.

- The panel says what it is helping with, what that part needs, questions to think about, and any problems the
  program's own checks found. This works even when AI is off, over budget or not consented.
- With AI on, ask one of three ready questions or type your own. The assistant sees what you have typed but not saved.
- **Suggest changes** gives change cards. A card only fills the form; nothing is saved until you press the page's own
  **Save**. **Dismiss** drops it.
- On the Assessment and Work Style pages, the assistant never suggests a score or picks a quiz answer for you.

**Obstacles.** In the obstacle form: **Who could help** and **Match my partners** (your own partners who fit), **Draft a
message to them** for the required partner, and with AI: **Suggest type and severity**, **Find the root cause** (up to
three "why" questions you answer in place), **What could I try?** and **Turn this into a task** (opens the task form,
filled, owned by you, dated within the step). On the conflict worksheet and criticism mining, **Help me think this
through** separates the useful point from the emotion; your private reaction is sent only if you tick it, for that one
request. Above the list: **Blocked tasks without an obstacle** (**Make this an obstacle**) and **Which obstacle should I
tackle first?**

**Communication.** **Help with this** on any field explains what it is for, with an example. In the message form:
**Check my message**, **Rebuild message**, and for a sent or replied message **Draft a follow-up** or **Thank and report
back** (both open a new message to the same partner). With AI: fill the empty fields from one sentence, change the tone
or language, **Prepare for objections**, **Practise this ask**, **Adapt to this partner** (uses the partner's work style
and motivator, never their contact details), **Read a reply** (paste a reply; it is never saved) and, after a reply
agreeing to meet, **Prepare for the meeting**. Above the list: **Messages to follow up** and **Which message should I send
first?**

**Modules.** **Which module should I do next?** and **How far have I come?** on the list. On a module: **Explain this
module**, answer boxes under the **Help me start** questions (used by **Draft my answer**), **Refine the draft** (rows you
changed are kept), **Check before I save**, and for modules done on another page, what to do there and whether it is done.

**Assessment.** **What does this mean?** under each statement, **What should I practise this week?** (one practice per
barrier scored 3 or less, with **Do it now** and **Add as a habit**), a reminder to retake 4 weeks after your last take
once you finish a module, and **What changed since last time?**

**Work Style.** **What does my result mean?**, **Put my style to work**, **Balance my partners** (with **Estimate their
style** for partners with none, **Working with them** and **Adapt my message**), and the switch **Let AI helpers use my
work style** (off until you turn it on; when on, only your main and second style are used).

Admins turn the page assistant and helpers off per page on **AI settings**, or for one cohort on **Cohorts**. A page that
is off still shows the program's own help. The admin **AI settings** page also shows **Page assistant quality**: requests,
cost, ratings, and how many suggestions were applied, edited or dismissed, per feature.

## Add Partners

Open `Partners` to create mentors, experts, advisors, colleagues, or resources. Partners can be linked to a Vision Area, Dream, Goal, Step, or Task.

## Track Obstacles

Open `Obstacles` to record blockers. Link each obstacle to the relevant dream, goal, step, or task. Assign a partner when support is needed.

## Build Communication

Open `Communication` to draft support requests. Select a partner and related work item, fill the purpose/request/outcome fields, then use `Generate message`.

## Review Progress

Open `Reviews` to record daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly reviews.

Useful review notes:
- what was completed
- what was delayed
- what is blocked
- lessons learned
- next actions

## Appearance

Open `Appearance` in the sidebar, or click the sun/moon icon in the header for
the same controls without leaving the page.

Everything here applies immediately and is saved to your account, so the look you
choose follows you to any browser or device you sign in from.

**Theme** — a preset sets the mode and accent together in one click:

| Preset | What it is |
|---|---|
| Fluent System | Follows your device's light/dark setting (the default) |
| Fluent Light | Always light, brand blue |
| Fluent Dark | Always dark, brand blue |
| Ocean | Light and teal |
| Forest | Light and green |
| Slate | Light and steel blue |
| Midnight | Dark and purple |

Change any individual control afterwards and the theme is shown as **Custom** —
the app won't keep a preset name that no longer describes what you're looking at.
Pick a preset again to go back to one.

**Mode** — Light, Dark, or System. System follows your device and updates live
when your device switches.

**Accent** — twelve colours. Every one has been checked for readable contrast in
both light and dark, so no choice can leave text hard to read.

**Background** — changes the page, cards, and sidebar together:

| Tone | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Neutral | The default |
| Warm | A softer, cream-tinted canvas |
| Cool | A slight blue cast |
| Soft | A greyer canvas, so cards stand out more |
| Tinted | Follows whichever accent you picked |
| Flat | No canvas step — borders separate the cards instead |

Every tone is checked to keep text readable, so none of them can make the app
hard to read. This control is switched off while High contrast is on, because
that mode deliberately uses pure white or black — turn High contrast off and your
chosen tone comes straight back.

**Interface style** — Classic or Modern. This changes the *shape* of the app,
not its colours, so it works with any theme, accent, or background you picked:

| Style | What changes |
|---|---|
| Classic | The original look — square corners, crisp borders, tight shadows, and a breadcrumb trail in the header |
| Modern | Rounded cards with soft, wide shadows; a faint tint of your accent across each card; pill-shaped navigation; a search box with its keyboard shortcut in the sidebar; and a header that greets you by name on the dashboard, shows your partners, and puts Export within reach |

Classic is the default, so nothing changes unless you pick Modern. Unlike the
Background control, this one stays available while High contrast is on — a
rounded corner can't make anything harder to read.

**Font** — the default is your device's own font and appears instantly. Public
Sans, Inter, DM Sans, and Nunito Sans download once, the first time you pick
them, and are stored with the app rather than fetched from the internet — so they
keep working offline. Each option is shown in its own typeface, so you can see
what you are choosing.

**Density** — Comfortable or Compact. Compact fits more rows on screen without
hiding anything.

**Text size** — Small, Medium, or Large. This scales all text proportionally,
which keeps the layout intact — unlike browser zoom.

**High contrast** — stronger text, borders, and focus rings. It works *with*
Light or Dark rather than replacing your choice. Status and priority colours keep
their meaning: Completed is still green, Blocked still orange, Critical still red.

**Reduce motion** — turns off animations. If your device already asks for reduced
motion, that continues to apply whether or not this is switched on.

The **Preview** panel on the settings page shows real badges, buttons, tables,
and progress bars — not mock-ups — so you can judge a choice before keeping it.
Use **Reset to defaults** to go back to the shipped appearance.

If a change can't reach your account (you're offline, for example), it still
applies on the device you're using and a notice explains that it wasn't saved.

## Excel

Open `Import / Export`.

Export downloads the current system data as an `.xlsx` workbook.

Import currently validates workbook structure and reports row counts. It does not yet create database records.
